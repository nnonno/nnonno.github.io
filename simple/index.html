About

I am an Applied Scientist at the AWS Generative AI Innovation Center, where I work on LLM inference efficiency, agent infrastructure, prompt caching and model routing. I care about making models cheaper and faster to serve without giving up correctness, and about measuring that carefully.

Before that, I built multilingual content-safety classifiers and responsible-AI guardrail models at Amazon Alexa, and did clinical NLP research at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus, contributing to knowledge-graph retrieval and clinical text summarization. I received my Ph.D. from Purdue University, advised by Prof. Jin Wei-Kocsis. My doctoral research covered decentralized and distributed machine learning (including ML systems for NASA spacecraft networks and distributed learning on embedded devices), security for cyber-physical systems, and blockchain-based computing.

Interests: LLM inference and serving · AI agents and evaluation · interpretability · distributed systems and HPC.
